Address Change Timeline

Changing your address is easier when you do it in the right order. This page gives you a who-to-notify list with a week-by-week timeline, plus what to have ready. Never write real account numbers into an AI chat — write “account on file” or “see statement” instead.

Who to notify and when

4 weeks before you move

  • U.S. Postal Service — File a change of address online at usps.com or at the post office. It costs a small fee online and forwards mail for up to 12 months. Do this early so nothing gets missed.
  • Employer — Payroll, W-2, and benefits mail
  • Insurance — Auto, renters/homeowners, health, life (update where you live and where your car is garaged)
  • Voter registration — Update if you moved to a new county or state
  • DMV — Driver’s license and vehicle registration (deadlines vary by state, often 10–30 days)

2 weeks before you move

  • Banks and credit unions — Statements, cards, and branch location (log in first; do not send account numbers to AI)
  • Credit card companies — Billing address
  • Subscription services — Streaming, meal kits, magazines, box subscriptions
  • Online retailers — Shipping address on file
  • Family, friends, doctors, dentists, schools

1 week before you move

  • Confirm your USPS change of address is active
  • Tell your landlord or property manager (written notice per your lease)
  • Update delivery addresses on frequently used shopping accounts

Moving day

  • Leave a forwarding address note for the new occupants or landlord, if allowed
  • Collect keys, garage openers, and access cards for the old place

After you move (within 30 days)

  • Register to vote at the new address
  • Update your driver’s license and vehicle registration if you moved states
  • Update insurance policies with the new address
  • Confirm mail is forwarding properly; check with the post office if anything is missing

What to have ready

  • New address and move-in date
  • Old address and move-out date
  • Phone number and email address
  • Account numbers (keep these on paper with you — never paste them into AI)

Using AI with address changes

AI can draft the notifications for you. Good prompts:

  • “Write a short email I can send to my magazine subscription telling them my address is changing on [date]. Keep it friendly and short.”
  • “Make me a checklist of everyone to notify when I move to a new state, in order of importance.”
  • “Write a one-paragraph note to my landlord giving 30 days’ notice to move out.”

Never paste real account numbers, credit card numbers, or ID photos into an AI chat.
