Privacy First

Before you do anything else with AI for your move, read this page. It takes two minutes, and it will keep your family safe.

The golden rule

Never scan or upload sensitive IDs or financial documents to an AI.

That means passports, driver’s licenses, state IDs, Social Security cards, birth certificates, marriage certificates, leases, mortgage documents, tax returns, bank statements, credit card statements, and anything with your Social Security number or account numbers on it. If a document could be used to steal your identity or access your money, do not feed it to an AI chatbot — not even one that promises it is private.

Why

AI companies may keep your uploads to train their models, and even careful companies can be breached. Once your ID or financial information is in a system you do not control, you cannot take it back. Identity theft is hard to undo. A little caution now saves a lot of trouble later.

What IS safe to share with AI

You can generally share with AI:

  • Moving checklists, timelines, and packing plans
  • Box contents and room names (“kitchen,” “bedroom 2”)
  • Utility company names (without account numbers)
  • General questions about how to move, change your address, or set up services
  • Your own writing, recipes, and routines
  • Photos of household items for an inventory (make sure no IDs, bank statements, or credit cards are visible in the background)

A good habit: the “stranger test”

Before you paste or upload anything, ask: “Would I show this to a stranger standing in line at the grocery store?” If the answer is no, do not share it with AI either.

Local-first is better

Whenever possible, use tools that run on your own computer instead of sending your data to a website (for example, a local AI app such as Ollama or LM Studio). This manual points you toward local options where they exist, but even with local tools, keep the golden rule: no sensitive IDs and no financial details.

Not legal or financial advice

This guide covers moving and relocation logistics only. It is not legal, financial, or medical advice. For decisions about leases, contracts, taxes, insurance, or health, talk to a licensed professional.

Remember: no sensitive IDs and no financial details in an AI chat — ever.
