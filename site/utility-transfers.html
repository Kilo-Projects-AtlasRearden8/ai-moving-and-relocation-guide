Utility Transfers

Turning services off at your old place and on at your new one takes a little planning. This page covers electricity, gas, water, internet, phone, and trash, with a timeline and what to have ready. Never paste account numbers or bill photos into an AI chat — write “account on file” instead.

What to transfer

Common services to move or set up:

  • Electricity
  • Natural gas or heating oil
  • Water, sewer, and trash
  • Internet and home phone
  • Cable or satellite TV (optional)

Timeline

4 weeks before you move

  • List every utility at your current address and your new address
  • Find each company’s phone number or website (old bills or a web search — do not upload bills to AI)
  • Ask your landlord or property manager which utilities you are responsible for

2 weeks before you move

  • Schedule shut-off at the old place for the day after you move out
  • Schedule turn-on at the new place for the day you move in (ideally one day early, so lights work when you arrive)
  • Ask if the new place needs a technician visit for internet or gas (book early — appointments fill up)

1 week before you move

  • Confirm shut-off and turn-on dates in writing (email or text to yourself)
  • Ask the companies if you need to be present for turn-on
  • Note any deposit or connection fee (write it in your budget)

Moving day

  • Take photos of each meter (electric, gas, water) at the old place before you leave
  • Take photos of each meter at the new place when you arrive
  • Read the meters and write down the numbers (date and time included)
  • Give forwarding address info to each utility company for your final bill

After you move

  • Pay final bills at the old address
  • Confirm new services are working (lights, heat, water, internet)
  • Update auto-pay accounts with the new address if needed

What to have ready (keep on paper, not in AI)

For each utility, have handy:

  • Company name and phone number
  • Account number (on file — do not paste into AI)
  • Service address (old and new)
  • Move-out date and move-in date
  • Whether you need to be present for turn-on

Using AI with utilities

AI can help you write the calls or emails. Good prompts:

  • “Write a short script for calling the electric company to move service to a new address. Include what info they will ask for.”
  • “Make a list of questions to ask an internet provider before signing up at a new address (speed, installation fee, contract length).”
  • “Write an email to my landlord asking which utilities I pay and which are included in rent.”

Never paste real account numbers, credit card numbers, or bill photos into an AI chat.
