Packing Checklists

Packing is easier when you break it into small steps. This page gives you a week-by-week moving timeline, a room-by-room packing list, an “essentials box” list, and things you should not pack. You can ask AI to turn any of these into a list for your specific home.

“Tip: Ask AI to make you a custom checklist: Make a 4-week packing checklist for a two-bedroom apartment with one cat. Keep it short.”

Moving timeline

8 weeks before you move

  • Sort one room at a time: keep, donate, sell, recycle, trash
  • Start using up food in the freezer and pantry
  • Research movers or truck rental (get written estimates)
  • Begin a room-by-room inventory

6 weeks before you move

  • Pack off-season clothes, books, and decorations
  • Label every box with the room and a short contents list
  • Start your address-change list
  • Begin gathering utility account info (account numbers stay out of AI — see Privacy First)

4 weeks before you move

  • Confirm movers or truck reservation
  • Pack rarely used rooms (guest room, attic, basement)
  • Schedule utility shut-off at the old place and turn-on at the new one
  • Request time off work for moving day

2 weeks before you move

  • Finish packing all non-essentials
  • Pack an “essentials box” (see below) and keep it with you
  • Confirm your change-of-address with the post office
  • Defrost and clean the fridge/freezer

1 week before you move

  • Pack daily essentials last
  • Confirm moving-day details with movers or friends helping you
  • Pack a suitcase for each person as if going on a 3-night trip
  • Do a final walk-through of each room

Moving day

  • Do a final sweep of every room, closet, and drawer
  • Take meter readings (electric, gas, water) before you leave
  • Lock windows and doors, leave keys as agreed
  • Do a walk-through of the new place before unloading

After you move

  • Unpack the essentials box first
  • Set up beds and basic kitchen items before anything else
  • Update your address where still needed
  • Register to vote and update your driver’s license if you moved to a new state

Room-by-room packing list

Kitchen

  • Pack dishes with paper or bubble wrap between plates
  • Wrap glasses and mugs individually
  • Small appliances: cord wrapped, packed with padding
  • Food: donate or toss open packages; move only unopened non-perishables
  • Label: “KITCHEN — FRAGILE”

Bedrooms

  • Clothes: leave on hangers and cover with garbage bags, or pack in suitcases
  • Jewelry and valuables: keep with you, not in the moving truck
  • Bedding: pack last, unpack first
  • Label: “BEDROOM — [primary / guest / kid]”

Bathroom

  • Toiletries: seal bottles with tape, pack in a waterproof bin
  • Medicines: keep with you
  • Old makeup and expired medicine: toss before you move
  • Label: “BATHROOM”

Living room

  • Books: pack in small boxes (books get heavy fast)
  • Electronics: photograph cable connections before unplugging
  • Label cords with masking tape (“TV,” “soundbar”)
  • Label: “LIVING ROOM”

Garage / storage

  • Dispose of paint, chemicals, propane, and batteries properly (movers won’t move them)
  • Drain gas from lawnmowers and grills
  • Tools: wrap sharp edges
  • Label: “GARAGE”

Essentials box (travels with you)

Pack these and carry them in your car, not the truck:

  • IDs, lease, and moving paperwork (paper copies, not uploaded to AI)
  • Medications and a first-aid kit
  • Phone chargers and a power bank
  • Basic tools (screwdriver, box cutter, tape)
  • Toilet paper, paper towels, hand soap
  • Snacks and water
  • A change of clothes and pajamas
  • Pet food and supplies, if you have pets
  • Basic cookware: one pot, one pan, plates, cups, utensils

What NOT to pack

  • Perishable food and open food containers
  • Plants (many movers won’t move them long distance)
  • Hazardous items: paint, chemicals, propane tanks, gasoline, batteries
  • Important documents you will need right away (keep these with you)
  • Anything irreplaceable: family photos, jewelry, heirlooms (carry these yourself)

Using AI with packing

AI is good at drafting lists, not at lifting boxes. Good prompts:

  • “Make a packing list for a studio apartment, one box per room, with what to pack first and what to pack last.”
  • “I have two weeks to pack a three-bedroom house. Make me a day-by-day schedule, one room per day, weekends off.”
  • “Write a label for each moving box: room on top, contents in the middle, fragile warning on the bottom.”

Never paste real account numbers, credit card numbers, or ID photos into an AI chat.
