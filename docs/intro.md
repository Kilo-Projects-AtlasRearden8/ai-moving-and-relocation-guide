# Getting Started with AI for Your Move

You do not need to be technical to use AI during a move. If you can open a website and type a sentence, you can do everything in this manual.

## What AI is, plainly

Think of AI as a very well-read assistant who has read a lot of text and can help you write, sort, plan, and brainstorm. It is not magic, and it is not always right. You are the boss. You check its work before you act on it — especially anything involving money, dates, or addresses.

## Where to start

You have three easy options, in order from simplest to most private:

1. **A free web chatbot** — Open a browser, sign up for a free account at a major AI provider, and start typing. Fastest way to try it. Not private enough for sensitive stuff. Keep to the golden rule: no sensitive IDs, no financial details.
2. **A local AI app** — Download a free program that runs AI on your own computer (for example, Ollama or LM Studio). Nothing leaves your machine. Slightly more setup, much more private.
3. **AI already in your tools** — Your phone, email, and calendar apps may already have AI features. Check your settings before installing anything new.

## How to talk to AI (prompts, plainly)

You do not need special commands. Just ask like you would ask a friend.

> "Help me make a simple 6-week moving checklist. Keep it short and doable."

Or:

> "I'm moving from an apartment to a house. Give me a room-by-room packing list for the kitchen, and tell me what to pack last."

If the answer is not quite right, say so:

> "That's too long. Make it shorter and add a step for changing my address with the post office."

## A simple starter routine

Pick one small task this week:

- Monday: Ask AI to draft your first-week packing list for one room.
- Wednesday: Ask AI to list who needs to know your new address and when to tell them.
- Friday: Ask AI to draft a moving budget with common expense categories (without entering real dollar amounts or account numbers).

Small wins build momentum. That is the whole idea.
