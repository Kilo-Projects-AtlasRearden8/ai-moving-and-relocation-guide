# Budget Tracking

A moving budget keeps surprises small. This page gives you common expense categories, a simple table you can fill in yourself, and ways AI can help you plan. All numbers below are examples only — replace them with your own. Never paste real bank account numbers, credit card numbers, or exact financial details into an AI chat.

## Common moving expense categories

- **Movers or truck rental** — company, deposit, mileage, fuel
- **Packing supplies** — boxes, tape, bubble wrap, markers, labels
- **Cleaning** — supplies or a cleaning service for the old place
- **Deposits and fees** — new rental deposit, utility connection fees, pet deposit
- **Travel** — gas, hotels, meals, flights
- **Storage** — temporary storage unit, if needed
- **New-home basics** — light bulbs, toilet paper, basic cookware
- **Insurance** — moving insurance or added coverage
- **Miscellaneous** — tips for movers, parking, tolls

## Track your spending

- Write each expense down the day it happens
- Compare actual to estimated at the end of the move
- Note what cost more or less than expected (helps next time)

## Using AI with your budget

AI is good at brainstorming categories and math, not at guarding your money. Good prompts:

> "List common hidden costs of moving I might forget (deposits, tolls, parking, cleaning fees)."
> "Help me write a one-sentence note to log each moving expense in a spreadsheet: date, category, amount, receipt saved."
> "I have $X budgeted for my move. Suggest low-cost packing supplies and free box sources."

Never paste real dollar amounts tied to your accounts, account numbers, credit card numbers, or bank statements into an AI chat. If you want AI to help with math, use made-up example numbers like the table above.
